Our story

From a Lagos shawarma spot to a growing food brand.

01 Shawarma started in Lagos with a simple focus: big, satisfying shawarma for people with serious cravings.

From those Lagos roots, it has grown into a brand serving customers across multiple locations — with shawarma, small chops, grills and drinks on the menu.

Headquartered in Surulere, the brand keeps ordering simple: send a message on WhatsApp and your food is on the way.
